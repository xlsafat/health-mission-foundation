Introduction

Health Mission Foundation ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of personal and health information we collect in the course of providing geriatric medicine and aged-care clinical services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and store it, and the rights individuals have in relation to their information.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), as well as applicable state health records legislation.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Identifying details such as name, date of birth, address and contact information
  • Health information, including medical history, diagnoses, medications, and assessment or treatment records
  • Information provided by referring GPs, hospitals, aged-care facilities, families and carers
  • Contact details submitted through our website enquiry form

How We Use Your Information

Personal and health information is used to:

  • Provide clinical assessment, treatment and ongoing care
  • Coordinate care with GPs, hospitals, aged-care facilities and allied health providers
  • Communicate with patients, families and carers about care
  • Meet our legal, clinical record-keeping and regulatory obligations
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through this website

Disclosure of Information

We only disclose personal and health information to third parties where necessary for a patient's care (for example, to a treating GP, hospital or allied health provider), where required or authorised by law, or with the individual's consent. We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal and health information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including through secure clinical record systems and restricted access to patient records.

Access & Correction

Individuals may request access to, or correction of, their personal and health information held by us, subject to any applicable legal exceptions. Requests can be made using the contact details below.

Website Enquiries

Information submitted through our website's contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not used for marketing purposes without consent. To safeguard your privacy, please do not submit personal or confidential medical information through the general website contact form — referrals should be sent through the appropriate secure electronic referral routes described on our Referrals page.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how your personal or health information has been handled, please contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) or the relevant state health complaints body.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date shown above.
