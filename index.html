About Us

Our Mission and Approach

We believe healthcare should focus on the whole person rather than only on individual diseases or medical conditions.

As people get older, health needs can become more complex and may include physical health, memory and cognition, medications, mobility, independence, and social circumstances. Our approach is grounded in comprehensive assessment, careful clinical decision-making, and partnership with patients, their families, and other healthcare providers.

We are committed to providing:

Person-centred and compassionate care
Comprehensive assessment of complex health needs
Evidence-based medical practice
Support for healthy ageing and independence
Collaborative care involving families, GPs and healthcare teams

We want to enable individuals to receive appropriate care at the right time while respecting their values, preferences, and goals.

Our Services

Specialist Geriatric & Community-Based Healthcare

The Health Mission Foundation offers thorough assessments and specialist medical care to older individuals and those with complex healthcare needs.

01

Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment

We develop an individualised care plan by reviewing the patient's medical conditions, medications, functional ability, mobility, cognitive function, psychological well-being, and social circumstances.

02

Healthy Ageing and Frailty

The evaluation and advice aim to maintain a person's health, independence, mobility, and quality of life, and to detect and manage frailty and age-related functional decline.

03

Cognitive and Memory Assessment

The evaluation and management of memory and other cognitive issues, including those related to dementia and other causes of cognitive impairment.

04

Capacity Assessment

The clinical evaluation of an individual's ability to make decisions when clinically sound and appropriate.

05

Dementia Care

The thorough assessment and management of individuals who have dementia, together with support and guidance for families, carers and healthcare providers.

06

Medication Review and Deprescribing

A careful medication review is carried out to improve treatment, reduce unnecessary drugs, and minimise harm, especially in older people with multiple health conditions.

07

Falls and Functional Assessment

An evaluation of factors leading to falls, decreased mobility, and functional decline, along with suggestions to support safety and independence.

08

Complex Medical Care

Specialist evaluation and care for older individuals with multiple medical conditions and complex healthcare needs.

09

Perioperative Medicine and Frailty Assessment

We evaluate and optimise older, medically complex patients, focusing on frailty, functional status, cognition, and medication management before and during surgery.

We currently offer our services by visiting aged-care homes and the residents of those homes.

Dr Elhassan Elabbas is a specialist in geriatric medicine and general medicine and is committed to giving high-quality, compassionate, person-centred healthcare.

Dr Elabbas has experience caring for older people and those with complex medical needs in both hospital and community settings. His professional interests include healthy ageing, frailty, comprehensive geriatric assessment, dementia care, medication optimisation and deprescribing, and perioperative medicine.

He believes good healthcare requires listening carefully to patients and their families, understanding a person's medical, functional, and personal circumstances, and working with general practitioners, families, carers, residential aged-care facilities, and multidisciplinary healthcare teams to ensure coordinated, individualised care.
